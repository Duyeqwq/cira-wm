# CIRA-WM Public Companion

Updated: 2026-10-27.

- Main research snapshot: `D-Star-Robotics/CIRA-WM`, private.
- Verified main source commit: `e5312d488c8034be940bc3fc82586e1f25eb4c3a`.
- This repository is a compact public companion: selected core method modules,
  standalone constraint checks, a project page, a project card and three MP4 previews.
- Complete backbone, training/campaign runtime, weights and data are not included.
- Open-loop evidence covers eight tasks and two training seeds.
- The long-bar pilot has only three independent physical initial states.
- Persistent constraints do not solve static/background drift.
- The larger two-task campaign is not presented as completed here.
- Videos are simulation/intervention previews, not CIRA prediction comparisons.
- The compact companion's 11 standalone CPU constraint tests passed; the static
  showcase also passed desktop, mobile and narrow-mobile playback/layout checks.
- No training, model inference, simulator rollout or hardware execution was
  performed to prepare this publication.

The complete recorded summaries are under `docs/evidence/`. Source and video
identities are in `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json`. Full details and reproduction assets
require access to the research repository and separately authorized data/weights.
